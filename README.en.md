## Materials and device foundations

A new [eight-week RRAM and sputtering course](courses/rram_materials/zh-TW/README.md) is available in Traditional Chinese. It starts with basic electricity, front-loads two weeks of lab preparation, and includes a [56-day study plan](courses/rram_materials/zh-TW/study-plan.pdf), editable slides, printable notes, separate exercises and solutions, and lab-record templates. Laboratory work requires local training and supervisor approval.

## Reading and printing ✏️

| What you want to do | Which PDF to choose |
|---|---|
| 📖 Read a whole course | Full handout, with linked contents, chapter bookmarks, and book page numbers |
| 🖨️ Print and write notes | Chapter handout, with two slides vertically on A4 |
| 🖥️ Read on screen or teach | Teaching slides, one 16:9 slide per page |
| ✍️ Work through problems | Open the exercises first, then the separate worked solutions |

Recalculate the intermediate steps in worked examples. For diagrams, identify what each node, arrow, and color means before checking the derivation.

### A few study routes 🧭

- **Programming and problem solving:** Basic programming → [Data Structures](courses/data_structures/en/README.md) → [Algorithms](courses/algorithms/en/README.md). Use Discrete Mathematics for proofs and counting.
- **Computers and systems:** [Computer Organization](courses/computer_organization/en/README.md) → [Operating Systems](courses/operating_systems/en/README.md). Start with instructions and memory, then processes, scheduling, and virtual memory.
- **Mathematics:** Study [Discrete Mathematics](courses/discrete_mathematics/en/README.md) and [Linear Algebra](courses/linear_algebra/en/README.md) at your own pace, returning to the tools you need.

## Books on the desk 📖

<details>
<summary>📚 Open the book list and scope notes</summary>

| Subject | Reference book | Authors |
|---|---|---|
| Algorithms | *Introduction to Algorithms*, 3rd Edition | Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, Stein |
| Discrete mathematics | *Discrete and Combinatorial Mathematics*, 5th Edition | Ralph P. Grimaldi |
| Linear algebra | *Introduction to Linear Algebra*, 5th Edition | Gilbert Strang |
| Computer organization | *Computer Organization and Design*, Revised Fourth Edition | Patterson, Hennessy |
| Operating systems | *Operating System Concepts*, 9th Edition | Silberschatz, Galvin, Gagne |
| Data structures | *Fundamentals of Data Structures in C++*, 2nd Edition | Horowitz, Sahni, Mehta |

Data Structures follows an eleven-chapter topic outline. The second-edition text was unavailable for section-by-section checking. The supplied Computer Organization reference does not include CD-only Appendices C, D, and E. Other scope notes are on each course's home page.

The books and scans are not included in this repository. Each full handout also lists its reference book, authors, and edition on the first page.
