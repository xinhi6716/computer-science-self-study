# Recursion, Recurrences, and Divide-and-Conquer

Start with binary search and powering, then study stable merge sort, recurrence proofs, maximum subarrays, and matrix multiplication. Work through the traces before using the general bounds.

[Teaching slides · 16:9](slides-teaching.pdf) · [A4 handout · two 4:3 pages per sheet](slides-handout.pdf)

[24 exercises](exercises.pdf) · [Worked solutions](solutions.pdf)

Contracts and termination; any-match versus first-match search; argument-copying costs; exact powering counts and Fibonacci bit costs; stable MERGE and peak space; substitution, recursion trees, the master theorem and rounding; nonempty maximum subarrays; all seven Strassen products and their reconstruction proofs. The Akra–Bazzi extension states its hypotheses and works through applications; its general theorem is cited.
