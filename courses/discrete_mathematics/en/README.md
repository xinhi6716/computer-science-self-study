# Discrete and Combinatorial Mathematics

20 chapter/appendix units with separate teaching material, exercises, and worked solutions.

[Course home](../README.md)

**[Download the full course handout](complete-handout.pdf)** (linked contents, chapter bookmarks, and book page numbers)

Teaching slides are for screen reading. Handouts place the same slides vertically on A4. Exercises and solutions are separate; attempt the exercises before opening the solutions.

Start with Chapters 1–5, then follow a theme. Counting, inclusion–exclusion, generating functions, and recurrences form one path; graphs, trees, optimization, and matching form another. Chapters 14–17 develop algebraic structures and applications. Textbook chapter numbers are retained, and the appendices can be consulted as needed.

**Themes:** [1. Foundations: counting, logic, sets, and integers](#foundations) · [2. Relations and automata: languages, states, and orders](#relations-machines) · [3. Combinatorial counting: inclusion–exclusion, generating functions, and recurrences](#counting-recurrences) · [4. Graphs and trees: connectivity, optimization, and matching](#graphs) · [5. Algebra and applications: rings, Boolean algebras, groups, and finite fields](#algebra) · [Reference appendices: exponentials, matrices, and infinite sets](#appendices)
