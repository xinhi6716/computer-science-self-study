# Operating Systems

20 chapter/appendix units with separate teaching material, exercises, and worked solutions.

[Course home](../README.md)

**[Download the full course handout](complete-handout.pdf)** (linked contents, chapter bookmarks, and book page numbers)

Teaching slides are for screen reading. Handouts place the same slides vertically on A4. Exercises and solutions are separate; attempt the exercises before opening the solutions.

Begin with kernels, system calls, and privilege boundaries in Chapters 1–2. Then follow processes and threads through synchronization, scheduling, and deadlock. Memory and storage cover addresses, faults, I/O, and persistence; protection and security ask who may perform each operation. Virtualization and distributed systems extend the execution environment. Revisit mechanisms through the Linux and Windows case studies in Chapters 18–19, then use Chapter 20 for design context. Textbook chapter numbers and stated version boundaries are retained.

**Themes:** [1. Kernels, interfaces, and system structure](#foundations) · [2. Processes, synchronization, and scheduling](#execution) · [3. Address translation and virtual memory](#memory) · [4. Storage, files, and I/O](#storage-io) · [5. Protection and security](#protection-security) · [6. Virtualization and distributed systems](#virtual-distributed) · [7. System case studies and design context](#system-cases)
