# Linear Algebra

12 chapter/appendix units with separate teaching material, exercises, and worked solutions.

[Course home](../README.md)

**[Download the full course handout](complete-handout.pdf)** (linked contents, chapter bookmarks, and book page numbers)

Teaching slides are for screen reading. Handouts place the same slides vertically on A4. Exercises and solutions are separate; attempt the exercises before opening the solutions.

Begin with vectors and linear equations, then use subspaces to understand solution structure and orthogonal projection. Determinants, eigenvalues, and SVD develop spectral methods; linear maps and complex matrices clarify coordinates and inner products. Applications, numerical methods, and statistics then put these tools to work. Original chapter numbers are retained for reference.

**Themes:** [1. Vectors and linear equations](#vectors-equations) · [2. Subspaces, orthogonality, and projection](#spaces-projections) · [3. Determinants, eigenvalues, and SVD](#spectral) · [4. Linear maps and complex matrices](#maps-complex) · [5. Applications, numerical methods, and statistics](#applications)
